# Team intro — what to say

For the Encode "Build on Arc" team-introduction video and the written team field.

**Name:** use the handle `geografinist` (also `orangterkucil` on some platforms). Do not
say your legal name on camera or write it in the form — the handle is what the GitHub
repo, the deployed app, and the socials are all under, so it is the one that actually
verifies you built this.

**Solo, and say so plainly.** A one-person team is not a weakness in a hackathon; trying
to sound like a company is. Judges have seen a hundred "we" videos from one person.

---

## On-camera script — 50 seconds

> Hi, I'm geografinist. I built FreelanceBot solo.
>
> I've freelanced, and the part that never got solved is trust before the work starts.
> The client doesn't want to pay someone they've never met. The freelancer doesn't want to
> work for someone who might vanish. So both sides stall, and platforms sell you an escrow
> that takes twenty percent and still takes two weeks to release.
>
> FreelanceBot puts the money in a USDC escrow on Arc, and puts an AI agent in the middle
> of the job — not as a chatbot on the side. The agent reviews the brief before it's
> posted, ranks the applicants, and when the freelancer delivers, it actually opens the
> file, looks at it, and writes an audit: what it verified on-chain, what it verified about
> the deliverable, and what is only its opinion.
>
> It does not hold the keys. It cannot move funds. A human still presses release — that's
> deliberate, and I wrote down why in the design docs.
>
> Everything you're about to see is on Arc testnet, with real transactions you can open in
> the explorer. Let me show you.

## Recording notes

- Look at the lens, not at your own face in the preview.
- The strongest line is "it does not hold the keys" — slow down there. It's the one thing
  that separates this from every "AI agent" demo that quietly has custody.
- Don't apologise for being one person, and don't say "just" a solo builder.
- One take, no cuts, is fine and reads as honest.
- If you fluff a line, pause two seconds and say it again — the pause is easy to trim.
