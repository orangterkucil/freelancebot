# Teleprompter — read this out loud, top to bottom

Only the spoken words are here. Open it on a second screen (or phone) and read
while you click. Sentences are kept short on purpose — easier to say cleanly, and
easier for a listener to follow.

**Pace:** pause at every line break. Don't rush. Total ≈ 3 minutes.
**If you fumble a line:** stop, pause two seconds, say it again. Cut it later.

---

## 1 · Landing page

> Freelancers do the work first, and get paid last.
>
> A designer in Jakarta finishes a three hundred dollar job for a client in New York.
>
> Two weeks later the money arrives — minus platform fees, minus the exchange spread, minus a wire fee.
>
> The work was done on day one. Only the payment was slow.
>
> FreelanceBot fixes that. It's a USDC escrow on Arc, verified by an AI agent, and it settles in under a second.

---

## 2 · Marketplace

> This is the open marketplace.
>
> Every job shows who posted it, and their rating — so you know who you're dealing with before you commit.
>
> Below that is live market activity. These are real escrows, moving from funded, to delivered, to paid.

---

## 3 · Client — post and fund

> I'm the client now.
>
> I post a job. A brief, a budget, and a deadline.

*(switch to freelancer, apply, come back)*

> A freelancer applied. I can see their trust score right here, before I accept.
>
> I accept them.
>
> Now I fund the escrow.
>
> This is a real transaction on Arc. The USDC gets locked in the smart contract.
>
> And the payout address is the freelancer's own wallet. I can't change it, and the platform can't either.

*(MetaMask opens — let it show, then confirm)*

> Signed. The escrow is funded.

---

## 4 · Freelancer — deliver

> Now I'm the freelancer.
>
> I deliver the actual work. I can upload the real files — not just paste a link.

*(drag the file in, wait for upload)*

> When I submit, I sign the delivery on-chain.
>
> Then the AI agent verifies it. It checks that the deliverable is reachable, that it met the deadline, and that it matches the brief.

*(wait for the verdict, then read what it says)*

> There's the verdict.

---

## 5 · Release — split screen

> The agent verified the work. But it does not move my money.
>
> That's on purpose.
>
> The agent is autonomous where it's safe — verification. It is not autonomous where it's dangerous.
>
> Only my signature releases the funds.
>
> So a prompt injection can't talk this agent into paying someone. It has no power to pay at all.

*(click Approve & release, MetaMask, confirm — then DON'T touch anything)*

> Released.
>
> And watch the freelancer's side. I'm not touching it.

*(wait — let the other window update by itself)*

> There. It updated on its own.
>
> Sub-second finality on Arc. Settled in USDC. A one percent fee, capped in the contract.

---

## 6 · Proof on Arcscan

*(open the transaction on Arcscan — hold here, don't rush)*

> Here's the transaction on Arc.
>
> That's the USDC landing in the freelancer's wallet. Real, on-chain, and anyone can verify it.

---

## 7 · Close

> The escrow contract is live on Arc testnet, and verified.
>
> The one percent fee is capped in the contract itself, so it can never be raised on a user.
>
> It's open source, MIT licensed, and it ships with an honest risks and security document — because this moves money, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.
>
> Arc public mainnet launches September sixteenth.
>
> FreelanceBot is ready for day one.
>
> Get paid the moment you deliver.

## If you'd rather not speak

A silent demo works too, and some judges prefer it. Same recording, but:

1. Don't narrate — just record the screen doing the flow.
2. In CapCut, add each line above as a **text overlay** at the matching moment.
3. Add quiet background music at low volume.

Keep the overlay text short — cut each line to its first half if it doesn't fit.
The three moments that must stay on screen either way: **MetaMask signing**, the
**freelancer window updating by itself**, and the **Arcscan transaction**.

## Pronunciation notes

- **USDC** — say the letters: "U-S-D-C"
- **Arc** — one syllable, like "ark"
- **Arcscan** — "ark-scan"
- **escrow** — "ESS-crow"
- **MIT** — say the letters: "M-I-T"

Say numbers as words: "three hundred dollars", "one percent", "September sixteenth".
