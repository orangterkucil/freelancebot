Solo builder · contract, app, agent

I freelanced before this, and the part that never got solved is trust before the work starts. The client won't pay someone they've never met. The freelancer won't start for someone who might vanish. So both sides stall — and the platform that breaks the tie charges 5–20% and still takes days to release.

I built the thing I wanted to exist. No co-founder, no team. The escrow contract, the app on top of it, and the agent layer that reviews the work are all mine.

Why the Accelerator: the hard part left isn't the code, it's everything around it — getting real freelancers and real clients to trust an escrow they didn't choose, and finding out where the agent's judgment is trusted and where it isn't. That's not something I can answer alone in a repo.

What I shipped

  • FreelanceEscrow.sol on Arc testnet — 1% fee, hard-capped in the contract
  • Next.js app — marketplace, escrow flow, two-sided ratings, file deliverables
  • Agent layer — brief review, applicant ranking, vision-backed delivery audit, daily sweep
  • Security pass — IDOR, SSRF, rate limits, fail-closed auth, two privacy leaks found and fixed
  • Docs — design decisions and known gaps, written down rather than hidden
